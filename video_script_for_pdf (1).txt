1. COVER (0:00 - 0:40)
Hello, my name is Nilamber Singh Hada. This is my SmartBridge Skill Wallet project on data analytics with Tableau.
The topic is Global Electric Vehicle Market Analysis. I used Python and SQL for the database, Tableau for the
visualizations, and Flask to show the dashboard and story on a website. On this first page you can see my GitHub link,
my Tableau dashboard link and my Tableau story link.

2. SECTION 1 - PROBLEM UNDERSTANDING (0:40 - 1:30)
Electric vehicles are becoming popular, but they are hard to compare. They differ in price, range, battery type,
charging time and sales. The problem is that buyers, manufacturers and planners cannot easily see which vehicles sell
the most, what drives the price, and which battery lasts longest. My objectives were to measure the market by category,
manufacturer and region, to compare battery technologies, and to show the results in a Tableau dashboard and story.

3. SECTION 2 - DATASET (1:30 - 2:20)
The dataset was provided by Skill Wallet. It has 100,000 rows and 37 columns. Each row is one electric vehicle model.
The model years are from 2018 to 2026. The columns include price, range, battery type, charging time, annual sales and
CO2 saved. A few columns have missing values, for example customer rating and resale value, but each is under 3.1 percent.
I left them blank so that averages ignore them. The data is synthetic, so some patterns are very even.

4. SECTIONS 3 AND 4 - DATABASE AND SQL (2:20 - 4:00)
I loaded the CSV file into a database table called ev_market using Python and pandas. It stores 100,000 rows. I used a
SQLite database, and the repository also has MySQL versions of the scripts.
(Figure 1)  Here you can see that 100,000 rows were stored, with 200 manufacturers and 15 categories.
(Figure 2)  This query shows the top 10 manufacturers by sales. SurgeWorks is first with about 4.88 million units.
(Figure 3)  This query shows sales and average price by category. Two-wheeler scooters sell the most, with about 85
            million units, and electric buses are the most expensive.
(Figure 4)  This query compares battery technologies. Solid-State batteries last longest, with 2,999 charge cycles and
            the lowest degradation, 7.36 percent. LFP is second.
(Figures 5 and 6)  These queries show regions and years. The regions and years are very even, because the data is synthetic.

5. SECTION 5 - TABLEAU CONNECTION AND PREPARATION (4:00 - 5:00)
I connected the dataset to Tableau Public. For data preparation, I left missing values blank, set a map role for the
manufacturing country, and used a Top N filter for the 200 manufacturers. I created calculated fields such as Price
Segment, Range Class, Fast Charge Rating, Range per 1000 dollars, Estimated Revenue and Sales Share. I also added six
filters: model year, market region, category, battery type, price segment and manufacturer.

6. SECTION 6 - VISUALIZATIONS (5:00 - 5:50)
I built 12 visualizations: sales by category, sales by year, top manufacturers, a country map, average price by region,
battery share, battery durability, price versus range, a drivetrain heat map, use cases, CO2 saved, and a customer rating
histogram. These sample charts show the main patterns.

7. SECTIONS 7 AND 8 - DASHBOARDS AND STORY (5:50 - 6:50)
The dashboards combine these charts and use the same filters. The size is set to Automatic, so the layout fits different
screens. The story has six scenes: market snapshot, who sells the most, price versus range, battery technology, regions
and countries, and recommendations. Both are published on Tableau Public.

8. SECTION 9 - FLASK WEBSITE (6:50 - 7:50)
Finally, I embedded the Tableau dashboard and story in a Flask website. This is the home page. This is the dashboard page,
with all the filters working. And this is the story page with the six scenes. The website runs on my computer at the
address 127.0.0.1, port 5000.

9. SECTIONS 10 AND 11 - PERFORMANCE AND INSIGHTS (7:50 - 9:00)
For performance, the project uses 100,000 rows, 6 filters, 6 calculated fields and 12 visualizations. My key findings are
these. Scooters, sedans and compact SUVs sell the most. Price follows battery size more than range. Solid-State and LFP
batteries last longest. Charging time and range are almost the same for all battery types. And the market is balanced
across regions and the top manufacturers.

10. SECTION 12 - CONCLUSION (9:00 - 9:30)
In conclusion, this project takes electric vehicle data from a CSV file into a database, analyses it with SQL, visualizes
it in Tableau, and publishes it on a website. In the future, I would add real-world data, sales forecasting, and a direct
MySQL connection in Tableau Desktop. Thank you for watching.
